    How it works

    1. Enter income and debts. Gross household income and recurring monthly debt payments drive the estimate.
    2. Add the down payment. The mortgage base is the purchase price minus your available down payment.
    3. Set mortgage assumptions. Interest rate and amortization are converted and applied consistently.
    4. Add ownership costs. Property tax, insurance, heating, and maintenance are added to the mortgage payment.
    5. Review the estimate. The app searches for the highest price that satisfies the affordability constraints.

    Assumptions and sources

    Model assumptions

    • Canadian mortgage rates are treated as nominal annual rates compounded semi-annually; an equivalent monthly rate is derived for calculations.
    • Mortgage default insurance is assumed to be financed into the mortgage balance when required.
    • Affordability thresholds use a GDS limit of 39% and TDS limit of 44% of gross monthly household income.
    • The mortgage stress test qualifying rate is the greater of the contract rate plus 2% or 5.25%.
    • Estimated affordable price is the highest price satisfying the enabled constraints within a configured search cap.
